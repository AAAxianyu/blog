## Why I Started This Blog

Welcome! After years of reading other people's blogs and benefiting from their knowledge, I've decided to start my own. This space will serve as a public notebook — a place to document what I learn, share ideas, and hopefully help others along the way.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about a range of topics that interest me:

- **Web Development** — Deep dives into React, Next.js, TypeScript, and modern frontend architecture
- **Software Design** — Patterns, principles, and the craft of writing maintainable code
- **Tools & Productivity** — Workflows, editor setups, and tools that make development enjoyable
- **Learning in Public** — Documenting my learning journey and projects

## A Bit About Me

I'm a software developer who believes in the power of clear writing and clear thinking. The act of putting thoughts into words forces clarity — and that's exactly what this blog is for.

I hope you find something useful here. Feel free to reach out — I'd love to connect!
